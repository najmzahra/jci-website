Who Are We ?

JCI TBS - EL Mourouj is a local organization of Junior Chamber International (JCI) dedicated to developing young leaders aged 18 to 40.

We believe in the power of young people to create positive change. Through our training programs, entrepreneurship projects, and international competitions, we develop the skills needed to build future leaders.

Discover Our Story

What We Do ?

JCI develops young leaders by providing opportunities for personal development, community impact, international cooperation, and entrepreneurship. Through hands-on projects and practical training, we prepare tomorrow's leaders to meet the challenges of an ever-changing world.
