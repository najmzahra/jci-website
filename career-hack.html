CareerHack Summit
V · The Path
Pillar I of IV · The Imperial Codex
Your CV.
Your first and sometimes only impression.
A recruiter spends 8 seconds on your CV.
In those 8 seconds, they decide if you are worth their time.
Most CVs say the same things.
Most CVs are forgotten the same way.
Yours does not have to be.
I
What You Must Do
→
One page. No exceptions. Length signals you don't know what matters.
→
Lead with impact, not duties. What you achieved, not what you were assigned.
→
Quantify everything. Numbers create proof. Words without numbers create noise.
→
Remove all fluff. "Passionate team player" means nothing. Results mean everything.
The Difference It Makes
A strong CV does not get you the job.
It gets you the conversation.
CareerHack Summit
VI · The CV
Pillar II of IV · The Imperial Codex
LinkedIn.
The arena where recruiters hunt.
Recruiters do not wait for applications.
They search. They filter. They reach out.
If your profile is empty or generic,
you do not exist to them.
30 days of intentional presence can change that.
II
What You Must Do
→
Professional photo. Profiles with photos get 14× more views. This is not optional.
→
Sharp headline. Not "Student at X University." Tell them what you do and what you want.
→
Post 3 times in 30 days on your domain. Insights. Projects. Lessons. Show you think.
→
Engage with industry content. One smart comment puts you in front of the right people.
The Difference It Makes
Visibility online means recruiters find you
before you even apply.
CareerHack Summit
VII · LinkedIn
Pillar III of IV · The Imperial Codex
Networking.
Most opportunities are never posted.
Jobs go to people someone already knows.
Internships go to students already in the room.
You have attended events.
You just didn't have a strategy.
One real conversation opens doors
that 100 applications cannot.
III
What You Must Do
→
Ask, don't pitch. Show genuine curiosity. People remember who made them think.
→
Prepare one question per person you want to meet. Specific questions signal seriousness.
→
Follow up within 24 hours. Most students never do. This alone sets you apart.
→
Be remembered for curiosity, not desperation. Confidence is the signal. Need is the noise.
The Difference It Makes
You stop being a name on a screen.
You become a person they remember.
CareerHack Summit
VIII · Networking
Pillar IV of IV · The Imperial Codex
Positioning.
A JACK OF ALL TRADES IS A MASTER OF NONE.
Recruiters hire specialists.
They want someone they can describe in one sentence.
If you try to appeal to everyone,
you are relevant to no one.
One clear domain makes you
the person others recommend by name.
IV
What You Must Do
→
Choose one domain. Data. Product. Finance. Supply chain. Pick the one that is yours.
→
Build visible expertise. Write about it. Share projects. Show your thinking publicly.
→
Become the go-to person in your circle for that domain. Then that reputation spreads.
→
Stay consistent. Positioning is built over months, not one post. Commit and repeat.
The Difference It Makes
You stop competing with everyone.
You start being chosen for something specific.
