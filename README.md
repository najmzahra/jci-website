# JCI TBS Club Website

A modern, responsive 6-page informational website for the JCI TBS (Junior Chamber International - Tunisia Business School) chapter. Inspired by the official JCI website (jci.cc).

## 🌐 Live Preview

Open `index.html` in your browser to view the website.

## 📁 Project Structure

```
jci/
├── index.html          # Home page
├── about.html          # About Us page
├── team.html           # Our Team (2026 Board) page
├── events.html           # News & Events page
├── partners.html       # Be a Partner page
├── contact.html        # Contact page
├── css/
│   └── style.css       # Main stylesheet
├── js/
│   └── main.js         # JavaScript functionality
├── images/             # Image assets (add your own)
└── README.md           # This file
```

## 📄 Pages Overview

### 1. Home (`index.html`)
- Hero banner with tagline and CTA buttons
- Quick intro about JCI TBS
- Four Areas of Opportunity section
- Featured news preview
- Team preview (4 key board members)
- Interactive Google Maps location
- Social media bar
- Newsletter signup

### 2. About Us (`about.html`)
- Mission & Vision statements
- Who We Are (JCI history, chapter story)
- JCI Creed
- Core Values
- Four Areas of Opportunity
- Chapter milestones timeline

### 3. Our Team (`team.html`)
- 2026 Executive Board (President, VP, Secretary, Treasurer)
- Board of Directors (8 department leads)
- Past Boards accordion
- Join CTA

### 4  . Be a Partner (`partners.html`)
- Partnership value proposition
- 6 benefits of partnering
- 3-tier partnership levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
- Current partners logos
- Partnership inquiry form
- Contact information

### 5. Contact (`contact.html`)
- Contact form with subject selection
- Contact information (location, email, phone)
- Social media links
- Interactive Google Maps
- FAQ accordion
